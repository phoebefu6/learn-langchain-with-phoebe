# Presenter notes - Leader session a1 · Chatbot, robot, or colleague? The autonomy ladder

Student-facing page: courses/a1-autonomy-ladder.html. These notes are for the presenter only.

## Preflight (do the night before, not the morning)

- [ ] Re-verify the Gartner agent-washing warning and cancellation forecast are still the latest published figures before any exec room (analyst numbers rotate; a stale stat costs credibility with this audience)
- [ ] Re-read Anthropic's "Building effective agents" definitions once - a leader WILL paraphrase them back wrongly and you need the exact line ready ("you fix the steps vs the model chooses the steps")
- [ ] Projector zoom tested (toolbar button); the ladder SVG must be readable from the back row
- [ ] Have one chat AI open and logged in for the sorting-partner prompt walkthrough (any assistant works - this track is tool-agnostic on purpose)
- [ ] Print or preload YOUR OWN 8-task portfolio sort as the worked example - leaders sort faster after seeing one honest example, including one task you yourself over-engineered
- [ ] Know which attendees have live vendor negotiations (ask the sponsor beforehand) - the agent-washing screen lands hardest when you can say "use this Thursday"
- [ ] If C-level in the room: rehearse the "approve the climb like you approve debt" line - it is the sentence they quote afterwards

## Run of show

| Min | Beat | Notes |
|-----|------|-------|
| 0-3 | Welcome + the premise | "Agents are a board topic; agent-washing makes due diligence YOUR job." Say it first - it justifies the whole hour to a skeptical exec |
| 3-8 | Part 1: ladder SVG + definitions card | The napkin drawing IS the deliverable; workflow vs agent in Anthropic's words, then the "who chooses the steps" refrain - repeat it three times minimum |
| 8-12 | RPA vs agents + hybrid pattern | The 70/30 split reassures automation owners in the room that their RPA estate is not dead - watch for visible relief, name it |
| 12-17 | Agent-washing card | Keep tone amused, not outraged; the one-question test out loud; tease Exercise 2 |
| 17-20 | Part 2: decision flow SVG + start-simple | "Add complexity only when it DEMONSTRABLY improves outcomes" - stress the word demonstrably; McKinsey's two lessons briefly |
| 20-32 | Exercise 1: sort your own portfolio | Everyone writes 8 real tasks; circulate; ask two volunteers for their circled "over-engineered one" - the confession moment carries the session |
| 32-40 | Exercise 2: the agent-washing screen | Run the 5 questions as roleplay - you play the evasive vendor, a volunteer asks; laughter here means learning |
| 40-45 | Q&A + homework pointer | Point at the data-team questions list: "pick one, ask it this week, that is the homework that matters" |

## Never-cut beats

1. Exercise 1 (the portfolio sort) - the session's value is leaders sorting THEIR OWN work onto the ladder; if time collapses, cut concept cards, never this
2. Exercise 2 (the agent-washing screen roleplay) - it is the take-home tool and the emotional peak
3. The "who chooses the steps" definition - every later session leans on it
4. "Add complexity only when it demonstrably improves outcomes" - the one rule the whole track hangs on

## Cuts if long

- The RPA hybrid card details (keep the 70/30 sentence, drop the insurance example)
- The self-study cost-intuition table - it is marked self-study for a reason; never present it
- Quiz can move to homework ("do it on the page tonight - it scores itself")

## Q&A landmines

- "So is ChatGPT an agent?" - Plain chat is a single LLM call; with tools enabled and the model choosing when to use them, it climbs the ladder. Nice ambassador for "it depends on who chooses the steps", not on the brand.
- "Our vendor says everything is agents now." - Exactly why the screen exists; do not disparage the vendor by name from the front of the room - hand them the five questions instead and offer to debrief after they use them.
- "Isn't this just anti-AI conservatism?" - No: start-simple is Anthropic's OWN advice to customers, and McKinsey's from 50+ builds. The people building agents for a living say agents aren't always the answer - skepticism about rungs is pro-shipping, not anti-AI.
- "Where do copilots/assistants fit on the ladder?" - Usually single call or workflow with a human in the loop by construction; that is why they shipped fastest. Good bridge to a2's HITL vocabulary.
- "Which rung is our project X?" (from someone senior, in public) - Do not sort a live political project from the stage; offer the decision flow and a 1:1. The ladder is a thinking tool, not a public verdict.
